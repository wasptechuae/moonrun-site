Moon Run: Armokh privacy policy

Last updated 22 November 2026 · WASP TECH, UAE ("we", "us")

Moon Run: Armokh is an endless runner game for iPhone and iPad. This policy explains what the game stores, what it shares and the choices you have. In short: your progress stays on your device and in your own iCloud, we run no servers and keep no accounts, and Google AdMob shows ads according to the choices you make.

Your progress, saved on your device

Your progress is stored in a file on your device, so the game works offline. This includes coins, gems, Moon Keys, heroes, gliders, upgrades, missions, achievements, best scores, your player name and your settings. It is not sent to us.

No accounts on our servers

We run no game servers and create no accounts. We never receive your progress, name, email or location.

iCloud key-value storage

If you are signed in to iCloud, the game keeps a copy of your progress in its private iCloud key-value storage in your own Apple account, so your progress follows you to your other devices. Apple stores this copy and we cannot read it. Apple's privacy policy applies.

Game Center

If you use Game Center, your scores (best score and Daily Eclipse Run) and achievements are sent to Apple's Game Center so you can compare them with friends. Apple handles this data under its own privacy policy. You can manage it in your device's Game Center settings.

Sign in with Apple (optional)

You never need to sign in to play. If you choose Sign in with Apple, Apple gives the game an anonymous user identifier. The game saves it only with your progress (on your device and in your iCloud) to link the two. We do not receive your password, and the game does not ask for your name or email.

Ads (Google AdMob)

The game is free and shows ads from Google AdMob. Rewarded ads, for example to revive or to collect a reward, play only when you tap to watch one. Occasional full-screen (interstitial) ads can appear between runs. To show and measure ads, Google's Mobile Ads SDK may collect and process:

  • the device's advertising identifier (IDFA), only if you allow tracking (see below). If you don't, ads are not personalised with it;
  • coarse (approximate) location derived from your IP address, used to choose ads suitable for your region;
  • ad interactions (ads shown and tapped) and device information such as model, system version and language;
  • diagnostics such as crash, performance and other SDK diagnostic data.

Google processes this data under its own policy: How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services. We do not sell your personal data, and we never receive the advertising identifier ourselves.

Your consent: Google UMP and App Tracking Transparency

  • Consent message (Google UMP). Where the law requires it (for example in the European Economic Area, the UK, Switzerland and some US states), the game first shows Google's consent message. There you can accept, refuse or choose purposes. You can change your choice at any time with the privacy choices option in Settings.
  • App Tracking Transparency (ATT). Next, iOS may ask whether the game may track you across other companies' apps and websites. If you choose "Ask App Not to Track", the advertising identifier is not shared and ads are not personalised with it. You can change this at any time in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.

Purchases

The game has no in-app purchases. You earn coins, gems and Moon Keys by playing, and they have no cash value.

Delete your data (Delete account)

In the game, open Settings → Delete account. After you confirm, the game erases your progress on this device and in iCloud and forgets the Sign in with Apple link. This cannot be undone. Apple manages Game Center scores and achievements; you can change them in your device's Game Center settings. To stop using Sign in with Apple with the game, go to iOS Settings → your name → Sign-In & Security → Sign in with Apple. Deleting the game from your device also removes the progress saved there.

Who the game is for

Moon Run: Armokh is made for everyone and is rated 9+ on the App Store for infrequent, mild cartoon or fantasy action: a runner is chased by a stone guardian, with no blood. The game is not designed primarily for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you are a parent and think your child's data reached us, contact us and we will help.

Security and retention

Your progress on the device is signed so it cannot be tampered with. Data in iCloud and Game Center stays until you delete it as described above. We keep no copies.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and its date, and the game will also tell you about important changes.
