FAQ

How do I play?

Swipe left or right to change lanes, up to jump and down to slide. Tap to switch between Moon and Sun: only the bridges and blocks of the current mode are solid. Collect coins, grab power-ups and stay ahead of the Guardian. You can replay the tutorial in Settings.

Will I lose my progress if I change phones?

No, as long as you are signed in to iCloud on both devices. The game keeps a copy of your progress in your iCloud and uses the most advanced copy. Sign in with Apple is optional.

How do I see leaderboards and achievements?

Sign in to Game Center in iOS Settings, then open Ranks or Trophies in the game.

Why do I see ads? Can I change my ad choices?

The game is free. Rewarded ads play only when you choose to watch one (for example to revive), and short full-screen ads can appear between runs. Change your consent with the privacy choices option in Settings, and tracking in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.

Are there in-app purchases?

No. You earn everything by playing.

How do I delete my data?

Settings → Delete account erases your progress on the device and in iCloud. See the privacy policy for details.

The game runs slowly on my device

Open Settings and choose a lower graphics quality. Turn on Reduce motion if you prefer calmer effects.
